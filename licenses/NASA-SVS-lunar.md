# NASA SVS lunar maps: source and rights review

Reviewed 2026-10-24 for AncientEarth.

Source: [CGI Moon Kit, NASA Scientific Visualization Studio, item 4720](https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/4720/).

The [SVS usage FAQ](https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/help/) states: “All of our content is in the public domain (unless otherwise noted)”. It expressly permits downloading, use and redistribution for any purpose. The selected standalone map files have no contrary copyright or restricted-use notice on the item page. This review covers these maps, not unrelated NASA branding, photographs of identifiable people, music or third-party materials.

The [NASA media guidelines](https://www.nasa.gov/nasa-brand-center/images-and-media/) also address texture maps and graphical simulations, require source acknowledgment, and prohibit implying NASA endorsement. No NASA logo, seal, person or promotional endorsement is included in these maps. Commercial redistribution and modification of the reviewed public-domain maps are permitted; retain this review and the credits with derivatives.

Credit: NASA's Scientific Visualization Studio; Ernie Wright (USRA). Source observations: Lunar Reconnaissance Orbiter, LROC WAC instrument team / Arizona State University, and LOLA instrument team. Scientist credited by the source page: Noah Petro (NASA/GSFC).

Selected source files:

- [2019 lunar color map, 8k TIFF](https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/vis/a000000/a004700/a004720/lroc_color_poles_8k.tif)
- [LOLA elevation map, unsigned TIFF](https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/vis/a000000/a004700/a004720/ldem_16_uint.tif)

File hashes, processing steps and runtime derivatives are recorded in the accompanying lunar asset provenance and `licenses/assets.json`. Scientific conventions and limitations are documented separately in the project's lunar rendering documentation. AncientEarth's processing and rendering are project work; they are not reviewed or endorsed by NASA.
